A short collection of things I keep relearning.

## Decide fast, reverse cheaply

Most decisions on a small project are two-way doors. Pick the obvious option,
write it down, and move on. The cost of deliberating usually exceeds the cost of
being wrong.

## Keep the diff small

A change you can read in one sitting is a change you can review, revert, and
reason about. When a branch sprawls, that's usually a sign it's really several
changes wearing a trenchcoat.

## Let the tools do the boring parts

Formatting, linting, deploys, and search-engine pings should all be automated.
If I'm doing it by hand more than twice, it goes in CI.

> The goal isn't to do more. It's to make the next change easy.
